Privacy Policy

Jumpy Dino (package com.sefemma.jumpydinogame), published by Sefemma Games. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (eggs, gold, unlocked dinos and items) and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional gold packs. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game only that a purchase was completed, using a purchase token, so the gold can be delivered once. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The game uses internet access only to talk to Google Play for in-app purchases.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
