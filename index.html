Privacy Policy

Jumpy Dino: Egg Rescue (package com.sefemma.jumpydinogame), published by Sefemma Games. Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Data we collect

We do not run our own servers and do not collect, store, share or sell personal information ourselves. The game includes two Google services, described below, which handle some data on their own terms.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (eggs, gold, unlocked dinos and items) and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads: you can choose to watch an ad to continue a run. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized.

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), the game asks for your choice through Google's consent tool before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional gold packs. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game only that a purchase was completed, using a purchase token, so the gold can be delivered once.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to talk to Google Play (purchases) and Google AdMob (ads). It uses the advertising ID permission for ads.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
