Privacy Policy

Jumpy Dino: Egg Rescue (package com.sefemma.jumpydinogame), published by Sefemma Games. Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Data we collect

This policy covers the game on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). We do not collect, share or sell personal information. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics (described below) and includes Google services, also described below, which handle some data on their own terms.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (eggs, gold, unlocked dinos and items) and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, the game sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog (servers in the United States). These events include things like when a session starts, how long you play, runs started and finished (height, eggs), which menus you open, which ads were shown and the estimated ad earnings, purchases of gold packs, and the game version, language, platform and approximate country (derived from your IP address, which is not stored by us). The events are linked only to a random identifier created by the game on your device. They do not include your name, email, account, advertising ID or device ID, and we do not sell or share them.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Anonymous stats. Uninstalling the game removes the random identifier.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (you can choose to watch an ad to continue a run or to double the eggs from a run) and occasionally shows a full-screen ad between runs. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized.

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), the game asks for your choice through Google's consent tool before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your achievements, leaderboard scores and an encrypted copy of your game progress (Saved Games) are stored by Google in your Play Games account, so they can follow you to a new device. Your Play Games profile name and avatar may be shown on leaderboards. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can manage or delete this data in the Google Play Games app.

Game Center (iOS)

On iPhone, achievements and leaderboard scores are stored by Apple in your Game Center account if you are signed in to Game Center. Your Game Center nickname may appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; you can manage it in your device's Settings → Game Center.

Ad tracking on iOS

On iPhone, the game asks for permission (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) before Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using the IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional gold packs. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play on Android and by the App Store on iOS. We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the game only that a purchase was completed, using a purchase or transaction ID, so the gold can be delivered once.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to talk to Google Play (purchases), Google AdMob (ads) and PostHog (anonymous statistics). It uses the advertising ID permission for ads.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
